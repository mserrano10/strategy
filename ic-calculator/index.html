7 · Context flags advisory only — none of these blocks a trade

Only four conditions block a trade and they all live in the calculator above: front-end ratio below 0.85, VXN below 25.2, credit under twice break-even at a fillable price, and a Monday gap of −2% or worse. Everything on this panel is weighed and adjusts size — the entire scheduled calendar accounts for only about ten per cent of historical damage, so it informs the position rather than deciding it. Two items here were never tested and are present because you should still look at them, not because the study can tell you what they mean.
