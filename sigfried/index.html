Core Investment Pillars

1. Elite Return on Capital

Truly great businesses generate high returns on invested capital with little incremental investment needed to grow.

2. Fair Price

Within a reasonable range, the price paid represents what we consider a fair intrinsic value of the business. Not bargains, but definitely avoiding overpaying.

3. Able Management

Led by people with the shareholder in mind, candor, and transparency. Unconcerned about anything else; not bonuses, stock performance, or vanity metrics.

4. Strict Quantitative Filtering

Filtering for size, liquidity, ignoring strange financials, and completely avoiding structural sectors like banking.

Sources of Structural Edge

  • Volatility Tolerance: Unconcerned with short-term volatility. It is simply the price of admission.
  • Unconstrained Time Horizon: Fully comfortable sitting on hands for extended periods and looking foolish when required. No quarterly earnings pressure or analyst metrics.
  • Performance Alignment: Zero management fees means performance is everything.
  • Skin in the Game: We invest our own capital alongside our thesis.
