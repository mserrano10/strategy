Atlas Wealth Management · Core Principles

Principles of Investing

The mandate of Atlas Wealth is the multi-decade preservation and compound expansion of real purchasing power, executed through business valuation rigor and rules-based risk control.

1. Ownership Over Speculation

Capital is allocated exclusively into productive assets with enduring economic moats, pricing power, and high return on invested capital (ROIC). Price fluctuations are treated as secondary noise; underlying earnings power is the sole measure of value creation.

Core Allocator Mandate

Do not trade business momentum. Acquire structural cash-flow streams at prices that offer an asymmetric margin of safety relative to conservative intrinsic value estimates.

2. Asymmetric Risk & Volatility Decoupling

Volatility is not risk; permanent loss of capital is. Market volatility is systematically harvested through rules-based derivative hedging rather than feared. Option strategies are structured to defined-risk bounds, insulating long equity ownership against catastrophic drawdown regimes.

3. Multi-Decade Horizon Discipline

Short-term performance benchmarks encourage structural fragility. Capital architecture must be designed to comfortably withstand deep market panics, liquidity crunches, and inflationary regimes without forced liquidations.
