1. The 2-Bullet Execution Triggers

To eliminate emotional decision-making during severe market panics, capital reserves are deployed into TQQQ in two distinct tranches based on high-water mark drawdowns in the underlying Nasdaq-100 index (QQQ):

  • Bullet 1 (50% Reserve Allocation): Executed when QQQ pulls back -10% from its trailing high-water mark peak.
  • Bullet 2 (50% Reserve Allocation): Executed if the drawdown deepens to -15% from the peak high-water mark.
