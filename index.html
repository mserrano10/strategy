    <h2>1. Executive Summary & Core Mandate</h2>

    <p>

        The <strong>2-Bullet Asymmetric Leveraged Equity Strategy</strong> is a rules-based, tail-risk exploitation engine. It converts macro equity market panics into non-linear portfolio compounding by systematically deploying cash into 3x daily leveraged NASDAQ-100 exposure (TQQQ) during structural drawdowns.

    </p>

    <p>

        To eliminate single-asset liquidation risk and smooth multi-decade equity curves, the strategy is constructed as a <strong>90/10 Hybrid Portfolio Architecture</strong>: 90% of overall net worth resides in core broad-market indexing (S&P 500 / SPY), while 10% is dedicated to the 2-Bullet Asymmetric Engine.

    </p>


    <div class="callout">

        <div class="callout-title">Core Strategic Directives</div>

        <ul>

            <li><strong>Hybrid Allocation:</strong> 90% Broad Market (S&P 500) / 10% Satellite Asymmetric Engine (0% intermediate rebalancing).</li>

            <li><strong>Engine Capital Split:</strong> 50% Bullet 1 (-50% Drawdown Trigger) / 50% Bullet 2 (-80% Drawdown Trigger).</li>

            <li><strong>Dry Powder Integrity:</strong> Uninvested satellite cash must reside exclusively in Treasury MMFs or Ultra-Short T-Bills. Equities or SPY derivatives are strictly prohibited as dry powder proxies due to crash impairment.</li>

            <li><strong>Risk Control Cap:</strong> Capping the engine at 10% isolates daily derivative leverage and overnight liquidation risks while adding 4%–6% in compound annual alpha.</li>

        </ul>

    </div>

    </p>

    <p>

        <strong>2. Dry Powder Impairment Protection:</strong> Keeping Bullet 2 in an S&P 500 index fund rather than MMF reduces final strategy wealth by ~40%. When TQQQ touches its -80% trigger, broad equities are down -25% to -50%, forcing forced liquidation of equities at market troughs.

    </p>
